why

      
the start is the whole game.

      

        
on almost every launchpad, every coin is born the same way. the same tiny market cap, the same curve, the same graduation point. a joke coin and a real project get the exact same first block, and that first block is where most of the damage happens.

        
at a tiny start, the price is so low that one fast wallet can buy a huge piece of the supply before a person has even seen the chart. bundles of bots do it every block. by the time the first real buyer arrives, a third of the coin can already be in a handful of wallets waiting to sell.

        
fl00r changes one number: where your coin starts. pick a higher floor and the same SOL buys a smaller, fairer slice. nothing else about the coin changes. the supply is the same, the curve shape is the same, and it still graduates straight into a Raydium pool when it fills.

      

      

        
for creators
start where your coin belongs.
  • pick any opening market cap, from about $2K up
  • launch on Raydium LaunchLab from your own wallet
  • no presale, no team allocation, no extra cost for a deeper start
  • graduates into a Raydium CPMM pool at the curve's last price

        
for traders
know the curve before you buy.
  • every coin's start, raise target and fill point are on-chain
  • the same shape at every size: it always fills at 5× its start
  • deeper starts are harder for bots to corner in the first block
  • trade it on Raydium the whole way, curve and pool

how it works

        
01
pick your fl00r
choose the opening market cap, from about $2K up to millions. the supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 and the curve shape is the same at every size. a deeper start is just a bigger market cap, not money anyone puts in.

        
02
it trades on its own curve
your coin launches on Raydium LaunchLab with a constant-product curve built from your start. 690,983,005 tokens (69.1%) are sold on the curve. people buy and sell on it from the first second.

        
03
it fills at 5×
the curve's last price is exactly 5× its first. it fills after raising about 1.55× your opening market cap. start at $20K and it fills around $100K.

        
04
it graduates into Raydium
when the last curve token sells, LaunchLab moves the raised SOL and the other 309,016,995 tokens into a Raydium CPMM pool at the curve's last price. no jump, no gap to snipe. from there it trades on Raydium like any other pair.

the curve
constant product, run by LaunchLab: x · y = k. the curve starts with 1.25 × start virtual SOL against 1,250,000,000 virtual tokens.

        
the fill
it sells 690,983,005 tokens. after that the price is (1.25 / 0.559)² = 5× the first. the raise target is 1.545 × start.

        
the pool
the raised SOL and the other 309,016,995 tokens open a Raydium CPMM pool at exactly the curve's last price, so there's no jump at graduation.

faq

      
questions.

      

        
is this Raydium?
no. fl00r is an independent front end. the coins are created by Raydium's public LaunchLab program, trade on its curve and graduate into a Raydium pool. fl00r isn't affiliated with Raydium.

        
does a deeper start cost me more?
no. the starting market cap is a number on the curve, not money you put in. you pay LaunchLab's normal creation cost plus any first buy you choose. the first buyers just get a smaller, fairer slice for each SOL.

        
what are the numbers?
supply 1,000,000,000. 690,983,005 tokens (69.1%) sold on the curve, 309,016,995 (30.9%) go into the Raydium pool. the raise target is about 1.545× your opening market cap, so the curve's last price is 5× its first.

        
what are the fees?
LaunchLab's own trading fee on the curve, read live from its config: loading…. after graduation, the Raydium pool's normal fees.

        
why is there a minimum start?
LaunchLab has a minimum raise target per launch. fl00r turns that into the lowest start it can offer, about —.

        
can I still start small?
yes. a shallow start fills fast and gives an open launch. a deep start is harder to snipe but needs more buying to fill. the site shows both before you sign.

        
where does my coin trade?
on Raydium LaunchLab's curve from the first second, and in a Raydium CPMM pool after it graduates. it shows up on raydium.io under its mint address.

        
what's the image link for?
the coin's metadata points at a small JSON file fl00r serves, which links your image. paste a direct link to a png, jpg or gif. keep it short: on-chain metadata links have a 200 character limit.

        
how does the coin list work?
every launch from fl00r adds a 0-SOL transfer to a public tag address. the list reads that address's history from the chain and shows each coin's live LaunchLab pool. no database.

        
is it safe?
memecoins are a game and most go to zero. start small. nothing here is financial advice. check every transaction in your wallet before you sign it.
